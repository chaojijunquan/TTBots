Privacy Policy隐私政策

Effective November 21, 2026生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日

This Privacy Policy explains how TTBots ("TTBots", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the TTBots website and app (the "Service"). It pays particular attention to data we receive from your Google Account when you connect Gmail.

Limited Use disclosure. TTBots' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture so we can create your account and show who is signed in.

Google user data from Gmail

Only if you choose to connect Gmail, and only to carry out what you ask a bot to do, TTBots may access:

  • Email messages and attachments, including sender, recipients, subject, date and body
  • Threads, labels and drafts
  • Email you ask a bot to send on your behalf

Content you give the Service

Messages you type to bots, game projects and assets you create, and AI model API keys you choose to add.

Technical information

Basic logs such as IP address, browser type, error reports and request timestamps, used to keep the Service secure and working.

2. Google permissions we request

We ask only for the permissions needed for the features you use. Google shows you each one before you approve.

Permission (scope)Why TTBots needs it
openid, email, profileSign you in and identify your account
gmail.readonlySearch, read and summarize your email when you ask
gmail.modifyCreate drafts, apply labels and archive messages when you ask
gmail.sendSend an email you have reviewed and approved

3. How we use Google user data

We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of TTBots that you request, such as searching, summarizing, drafting, labeling and sending email. Specifically:

  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for advertising, including personalized, retargeted or interest-based ads.
  • We do not use Google user data to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data, or any data derived from it, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google user data, unless you give us explicit permission for specific messages (for example, to fix a problem you report), it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it is required to comply with applicable law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.
  • A bot never sends email without your explicit approval.

4. AI processing

To answer a request, TTBots sends the relevant content, such as the email you asked about and your instruction, to an AI model provider that generates the response. If you add your own model key, the request goes to the provider you chose. We use providers only to deliver the feature you requested, and we choose providers whose terms prohibit using this content to train their models. Model output can be inaccurate, so please review drafts before approving them.

5. How we share information

We share Google user data only as needed to provide the features you request, and never for any other purpose:

  • Service providers that host and run TTBots, such as cloud hosting and AI model providers, under agreements that limit their use of the data to providing the Service.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or others.
  • Business transfers, if TTBots is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to your data, and we will notify you first.

6. Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and never shown to other users.
  • Email content is fetched when a bot needs it to complete your request. We do not keep a permanent copy of your mailbox; temporary processing copies are deleted within 24 hours.
  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access to production systems is limited to what is needed to operate the Service.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time in TTBots under Settings, Connections, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When you disconnect in TTBots, we revoke and delete your tokens and any stored Gmail data right away.
  • Delete your account in Settings or by emailing us. We delete your account data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Request deletion of specific data by emailing chaojijunquan@gmail.com.

8. Cookies

We use only the cookies and local storage needed to keep you signed in and remember settings such as your language. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Email us to make a request and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

TTBots is not intended for children under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International users

TTBots may process data in countries other than where you live. We protect your data as described in this policy wherever it is processed.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use Google user data, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before using your data in a new way. For other changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify you in the app for significant ones.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: chaojijunquan@gmail.com

本隐私政策说明 TTBots（以下简称“TTBots”“我们”）在你使用 TTBots 网站和应用（以下简称“本服务”）时，如何收集、使用、存储和分享信息，并重点说明你连接 Gmail 后我们从你的 Google 账号获得的数据。

有限使用声明。TTBots 对通过 Google API 获得的信息的使用，以及向任何其他应用的传输，将遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”要求。

1. 我们收集的信息

账号信息

你使用 Google 登录时，我们会获得你的姓名、邮箱地址和头像，用于创建账号和显示当前登录的用户。

来自 Gmail 的 Google 用户数据

只有在你主动连接 Gmail 后，并且只为了完成你交给机器人的任务，TTBots 才可能访问：

  • 邮件和附件，包括发件人、收件人、主题、日期和正文
  • 邮件会话、标签和草稿
  • 你让机器人代你发送的邮件

你提供给本服务的内容

你发给机器人的消息、你创建的游戏项目和素材，以及你选择添加的 AI 模型 API 密钥。

技术信息

IP 地址、浏览器类型、错误报告和请求时间等基础日志，用于保障本服务安全、正常运行。

2. 我们申请的 Google 权限

我们只申请你所用功能必需的权限。授权前，Google 会逐项向你展示。

权限范围（scope）TTBots 的用途
openid、email、profile登录并识别你的账号
gmail.readonly按你的要求搜索、读取和总结邮件
gmail.modify按你的要求创建草稿、添加标签和归档邮件
gmail.send发送你已检查并确认的邮件

3. 我们如何使用 Google 用户数据

我们只将 Google 用户数据用于提供和改进你所请求的 TTBots 面向用户的功能，例如搜索、总结、起草、标记和发送邮件。具体而言：

  • 我们不出售 Google 用户数据。
  • 我们不将 Google 用户数据用于或转移用于广告，包括个性化广告、再营销广告或基于兴趣的广告。
  • 我们不将 Google 用户数据用于评估信用或借贷用途。
  • 我们不使用 Google 用户数据或由其衍生的任何数据，来开发、改进或训练通用的或非个性化的 AI 或机器学习模型。
  • 除非你明确允许我们查看特定邮件（例如排查你反馈的问题）、出于安全目的（例如调查滥用行为）、为遵守适用法律所必需，或数据已经汇总并匿名化用于内部运营，否则我们不允许任何人阅读你的 Google 用户数据。
  • 没有你的明确确认，机器人绝不会发送任何邮件。

4. AI 处理

为了回应你的请求，TTBots 会把相关内容（例如你询问的邮件和你的指令）发送给 AI 模型服务商来生成回答。如果你添加了自己的模型密钥，请求会发送到你选择的服务商。我们只为提供你请求的功能而使用这些服务商，并选择条款禁止用这些内容训练模型的服务商。AI 生成的内容可能不准确，请在确认前检查草稿。

5. 我们如何分享信息

我们只在提供你所请求功能的必要范围内分享 Google 用户数据，绝不用于其他目的：

  • 服务提供商：托管和运行 TTBots 的服务商，例如云服务器和 AI 模型服务商。我们与其约定，他们只能为提供本服务而使用数据。
  • 法律原因：法律要求时，或为保护用户及他人的权利、安全时。
  • 业务转让：如果 TTBots 涉及合并、收购或资产出售，本政策将继续适用于你的数据，并且我们会事先通知你。

6. 存储与安全

  • OAuth 访问令牌和刷新令牌加密存储，绝不会向其他用户展示。
  • 只有当机器人需要完成你的请求时才会获取邮件内容。我们不会永久保存你的邮箱副本，处理过程中产生的临时副本会在 24 小时内删除。
  • 所有数据都通过加密连接（HTTPS/TLS）传输。
  • 对生产系统的访问权限仅限于运营本服务所必需的范围。

7. 保留与删除

  • 断开 Gmail：随时可以在 TTBots 的“设置 → 连接”中断开，或前往 myaccount.google.com/permissions 移除。在 TTBots 中断开后，我们会立即撤销并删除你的令牌和已存储的 Gmail 数据。
  • 删除账号：在设置中操作或发邮件给我们。我们会在 30 天内删除你的账号数据，法律要求保留的除外。
  • 删除特定数据：发邮件至 chaojijunquan@gmail.com。

8. Cookie

我们只使用保持登录状态和记住设置（例如语言）所必需的 Cookie 和本地存储，不使用广告 Cookie 或跨站追踪 Cookie。

9. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能有权访问、更正、导出或删除你的个人信息，或反对某些处理。发邮件给我们提出请求，我们会在 30 天内答复。

10. 儿童

TTBots 不面向 13 岁以下（欧洲经济区为 16 岁以下）的儿童，我们不会有意收集他们的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童向我们提供了信息，请联系我们，我们会将其删除。

11. 国际用户

TTBots 可能在你所在国家以外的地方处理数据。无论在哪里处理，我们都会按本政策保护你的数据。

12. 政策变更

如果我们改变 Google 用户数据的使用方式，会先更新本政策，并在以新方式使用你的数据前征得你的同意。其他变更会更新本页顶部的日期，重大变更会在应用内通知你。

13. 联系我们

如有问题或请求，请联系：chaojijunquan@gmail.com
